English Grammar Lesson

Learn English grammar and vocabulary before playing the Match Word Game.

🟢 Easy Level

Learn basic English grammar and common vocabulary. This level is suitable for beginners.

1. Nouns

A noun is a word that names a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

👨 Person: Teacher

🏫 Place: School

📚 Thing: Book

🐶 Animal: Dog

🏠 Place: House

2. Verbs

A verb describes an action or something that happens.

Run - to move quickly using your legs.

Eat - to put food into your mouth.

Read - to understand written words.

Write - to put words on paper.

Learn - to gain knowledge or a skill.

3. Adjectives

An adjective describes a noun. It tells us more about a person, place, animal, or thing.

🍎 A red apple.

🏠 A big house.

📖 An interesting book.

👨 A kind teacher.

4. Pronouns

A pronoun is a word that can be used instead of a noun.

I - the person speaking.

You - the person being spoken to.

He - a male person.

She - a female person.

They - more than one person.

🟡 Medium Level

Learn more grammar rules, sentence structures, verb tenses, and useful vocabulary.

1. Present Simple

The present simple is used for habits, routines, and facts.

✅ I play football every day.

✅ She plays football every day.

✅ They study English.

❌ She play football.

2. Past Simple

The past simple describes actions that happened in the past.

Yesterday, I played football.

She visited her friend.

They watched a movie.

He went to school.

3. Future with "Will"

Use will to talk about future actions or decisions.

I will study tomorrow.

She will visit her family.

They will play the game.

4. Singular and Plural

Singular means one. Plural means more than one.

One book → Two books

One car → Two cars

One child → Two children

One person → Two people

5. Prepositions

A preposition shows the relationship between words, especially location or time.

The book is on the table.

The cat is under the chair.

She is in the classroom.

We go to school at 8:00.

6. Articles: A, An, The

A and an are used for singular nouns. The refers to something specific.

I have a book.

She eats an apple.

Please open the door.

🔴 Hard Level

Challenge yourself with advanced grammar, sentence structure, and more difficult vocabulary.

1. Subject-Verb Agreement

The subject and verb must agree in number. Singular subjects usually take singular verbs.

✅ The student studies English.

✅ The students study English.

❌ The student study English.

2. Comparative Adjectives

Comparative adjectives are used to compare two people, places, animals, or things.

John is taller than Peter.

This book is more interesting than that book.

My car is faster than yours.

3. Superlative Adjectives

Superlative adjectives compare three or more people, places, animals, or things.

Mount Everest is the highest mountain.

She is the smartest student in the class.

This is the most interesting book.

4. Adverbs

An adverb describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb. It often tells us how, when, or where something happens.

She runs quickly.

He speaks English fluently.

They arrived early.

She answered the question correctly.

5. Conjunctions

A conjunction connects words, phrases, or sentences.

I like tea and coffee.

She is tired but she continues working.

You can stay here or go home.

I stayed home because it was raining.

6. First Conditional

The first conditional is used to talk about possible future situations and their results.

If it rains, I will stay home.

If you study, you will pass the test.

If she works hard, she will succeed.

7. Passive Voice

The passive voice focuses on the action or the person/thing receiving the action.

Active: The teacher explains the lesson.

Passive: The lesson is explained by the teacher.

Active: The student wrote the letter.

Passive: The letter was written by the student.
